BRUSSELS — Leaders leave early as the EU’s €2 trillion budget talks collapse in the rain.

EU BUDGET TALKS COLLAPSE

Summit ends early, Friday’s talks cancelled. EU leaders fail to agree on the 2028–2034 budget of about €2 trillion and call off Friday’s talks; a €45 billion loan for Ukraine is stuck with it. Germany’s Merz says there is still “too much work”; Italy’s Meloni calls a deal “light years away”.

Transport · London

Tube strike begins, day 1 of 8

London Tube drivers start the first of 8 days of winter strikes at 01:53 on Friday, bringing major disruption across the network.

Markets · Tokyo

Nikkei +0.7%, Kospi +2% on Friday

Asian markets rise on Friday; Brent crude hits $96.14 and Tokyo core inflation reaches a 16-month high, backing a Bank of Japan rate rise as soon as next month.

War · Kuwait · Thu 26 Nov

Drone kills 2 Italian technicians

Two Rheinmetall technicians are killed as a drone hits a Kuwait air-defence station; Kuwait and Italy blame Iran, and Tehran calls the incident “suspicious”.

War · Jazan, Saudi Arabia · Thu 26 Nov

Houthi strike halts refinery

A Houthi attack on the Jazan refinery kills 1 Nepalese worker and halts operations, in the latest strike on Saudi energy sites.

War · Kyiv · Thu 26 Nov

Zelensky warns of mass strike on power grid

Ukrainian intelligence says Russia is finishing preparations for a major strike on the country’s energy infrastructure in the coming days.

Europe · Brussels

Leaders set 17–18 December for a second try

The next attempt comes at the 17–18 December summit; Merz ties budget approval to all 27 states funding Ukraine, opposed by Czechia, Slovakia and Hungary.
